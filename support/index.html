Common questions

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your phone. Selfie Note has no account and no servers, so we cannot see or recover your memories.

I lost my memories after changing phones.

Because nothing is uploaded, memories move only through your phone’s own device back-up or transfer tools.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Open Settings in the app and use the Reminder section. You can also change notification permission in your phone’s system settings.

How do I delete a memory?

Open the memory from History and choose delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

The camera does not open.

Check that camera permission is allowed for Selfie Note in your phone’s settings. You can also pick an existing photo instead.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026
Publisher: Seeroo IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (“we”, “us”)
Contact: hostingsupportseeroo@gmail.com

Selfie Note is a private, offline app for keeping one selfie and one short note a day. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your photos and notes stay on your phone, and we never receive them.

What we collect

Nothing. Selfie Note has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, and no third-party analytics or advertising services are built into the app.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your daily photos (taken with the camera or chosen from your photos) and the notes shown with them.
  • Your settings: theme, reminder time, sound on/off, and whether you have seen the intro.

All of this is saved only in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. We cannot see it, recover it, or restore it for you. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted permanently. Back-ups made by your phone’s own system (for example Android or iCloud device back-up) are controlled by your device settings, not by us.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — to take your daily selfie. The photo is saved on your device only.
  • Photos / media — only when you choose to pick an existing picture. The app reads just the picture you select.
  • Notifications — optional. Used to show a daily reminder at the time you choose. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no notification server is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in system settings.

The app does not use your microphone, location, contacts or any advertising identifier.

Sharing

The app can create a share image of a memory. It is shared only when you choose to and only through your phone’s standard share menu, to the app you pick. We do not receive it.

Children

Selfie Note does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Your choices and control

You can delete a memory inside the app, or remove all app data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in system settings. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the effective date, and the app’s store listing before the change ships.
